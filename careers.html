HOTEL GAMES // DEVELOPER DOCUMENT HG-001

The Developer's Creed

My passion is my purpose.

My ecosystem pays it forward. What I build should create opportunities for the people, creators and communities around it.

I don't want to sell you my product. I want you to want to buy my product before I walk through the door. The work should create its own demand.

The only barrier to my success is myself. My daily habits shape my health, wealth and mood. I take responsibility for improving what is mine to improve.

Coding and debugging may be frustrating at times, but we are not the first builders to face difficult problems. Frustration is part of development, not the end of it.

Developing competent modelers, animators and subdevelopers takes time. People over projects should guide our human-resource development.

The doctor called: accepting criticism constructively is necessary for self-development and growth.

Capture the idea. Write it down. Then execute. Remember the excitement you felt when you first thought of it. Do not let hesitation, fear, or self-doubt stand between an idea and its execution.

I will respect the player's time, intelligence and imagination. I will question systems, challenge assumptions and search for better solutions.

I will remember that every vehicle, interface, sound, character, mission, line of code, and environment works together to make our world feel alive.

I will share what I know, learn from those around me, and work with my team to turn great ideas into finished games.

I am not here simply to make software. I am here to build worlds.
